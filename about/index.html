About SimSpec Golf

The Golf Simulator Database helps home simulator builders research equipment and find courses.

Affiliate disclosure

Links may earn a commission. Buy buttons currently use manufacturer URLs as placeholders until affiliate links are available. Any sponsored placement will be labeled sponsored.

Our methodology

We use manufacturer product pages, manuals and official support documents for equipment facts. Each product lists its sources and verification date. Unverified fields remain blank in the data and display as “Not listed by the manufacturer.” Seed records carry a verification notice and example prices.

Prices reflect manufacturer list prices on the recorded verification date. Subscription costs describe the cheapest plan that unlocks the headline features. We distinguish measured parameters from calculated metrics when the manufacturer does so. Compatibility and space requirements require official sources.

Three-year estimates add hardware price to three annual subscription payments. Unknown subscription costs leave totals unlisted. These estimates exclude taxes, accessories and other software. Before promoting the site, our QA process calls for a source recheck of 20 percent of fields.

Course names, difficulty, location and designer come from the pakgolfstudios GSPro course list, fetched October 25, 2026 (3,086 courses). Par and rating are not published in that list, so those fields stay unlisted until a sourced dataset covers them.
