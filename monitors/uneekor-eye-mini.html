Subscription and three-year cost

Subscription required: No
Plan: Pro Package (cheapest tier with the third party software connector)
Annual subscription: $199.00

Player tier is free and included, with ball data, club data, VIEW range, and 100 Power U reports. Pro at $199 per year adds the third party connector that GSPro and E6 Connect require, plus Refine with 5 courses. Champion costs $399 per year and Ultimate costs $599 per year. A Pro Plus tier at $299 per year bundling a GSPro year has also been listed. GSPro itself costs $250 per year paid to GSPro. Uneekor publishes one 19 point data list per model. The measured and calculated split in this record separates direct reads from derived flight outputs. Club data requires club stickers, 1,280 included.

Three-year total with the optional plan above: $3,596.00
