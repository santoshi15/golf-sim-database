Subscription required: No

Annual subscription: $0.00

No annual subscription for the FS Golf app or data. The Pro Package is a one time $1,000 add on (listed at $699 on sale on flightscope.com on 2026-10-25) that unlocks 11 additional D Plane club parameters including club path, face angle, face to path, angle of attack, dynamic loft, and low point. Face Impact Location is a one time $499 add on and requires the Pro Package. Indoor spin readings need metallic stickers on the ball or Titleist RCT balls. A 12 course E6 Connect bundle for iOS and PC is included with purchase.
