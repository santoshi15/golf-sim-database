ProTee VX

Reverified on 2026-10-25 against ProTee's own pages only, after QA found the previous version of this record cited retailer golfbays.com for its product facts. ProTee publishes no price and no measured or calculated parameter list on its own site, so priceUSD, measuredParameters, and calculatedParameters are set to null. The $6,500 price and the parameter lists carried before came from retailer pages. E6 compatibility is set to null because the ProTee pages checked for this record do not state it. Placement and space fields come from ProTee's official VX setup guide.

ProTee does not publish a price for the VX on its own site. The VX is sold through authorized resellers, and ProTee Labs is activated with a code supplied by the reseller. US resellers listed the VX at $6,500 during verification. That figure is not used as the price here because the verification protocol counts manufacturer published prices only.
