Subscription and three-year cost

Subscription required: No
Plan: Foresight+ Gold (needed for the GSPro route for buyers without a legacy FSX license)
Annual subscription: $599.00

Ball and club data need no subscription. Foresight+ tiers: Free $0, Silver $199 per year, Gold $599 per year, with a Course Pass at $295 per year for the premium course library inside Foresight Premier. GSPro requires FSX Play or FSX 2020 as the bridge, or an active Gold membership for buyers without a legacy license. Base price excludes the Clubhead Measurement upgrade and the Essential Putting Analysis add on at $2,500, which unlocks the full measured club set and putting data.

Three-year total with the optional plan above: $21,796.00
