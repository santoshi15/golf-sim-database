Subscription and three-year cost

Subscription required: Yes
Plan: Essential (cheapest plan that unlocks third party simulator connections)
Annual subscription: $99.99

With no membership the unit runs the SkyTrak driving range and owners keep ball and club data. SkyTrak states owners cannot connect third party simulator software without a plan. Essential costs $99.99 per year. Core with the Foresight course pack costs $299.99 per year, Core with the TrackMan course pack costs $349.99 per year, and Elite with both packs costs $599.99 per year. Plan names and prices were restructured over the product life; earlier names were Game Improvement and Play and Improve. Plan prices here follow the SkyTrak plans page opened on 2026-10-25: Essential $99.99, Core Foresight $299.99, Core TrackMan $349.99, Elite $599.99. The required flag follows SkyTrak's own FAQ, which states a paid membership is required to use all features and to connect third party simulator software; basic range use is free.
