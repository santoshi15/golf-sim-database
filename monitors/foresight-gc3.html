Subscription and three-year cost

Subscription required: No
Plan: Foresight+ Gold (needed for the GSPro route for buyers without a legacy FSX license)
Annual subscription: $599.00

Ball and club data need no subscription, and current US pricing includes FSX 2020 and FSX Play perpetual licenses. Foresight+ tiers: Free $0, Silver $199 per year for club data unlocks on Bushnell hardware, Gold $599 per year. A Course Pass at $295 per year adds the premium course library inside Foresight Premier and stacks on Silver or Gold. GSPro on a GC3 requires FSX Play or FSX 2020 running as the bridge, or an active Gold membership for buyers without a legacy license. Club data uses a single fiducial marker on the club.

Three-year total with the optional plan above: $8,796.00
